## Information We Collect

{{siteName}} processes only the personal information required to prepare insured deliveries, maintain accurate gemstone appraisals, verify account access, and respond to private consultations:

- **Account & Order Details:** Name, email address, delivery and billing addresses, contact telephone number, and saved wishlist or bag selections.
- **Verification & Security:** Encrypted session identifiers and security audit timestamps used exclusively to prevent unauthorised access and fraud.
- **Payment Processing:** Card and UPI payments execute directly through PCI-compliant payment providers (Stripe and Razorpay). Raw card numbers are never transmitted to or retained on {{siteName}} servers.

## Optional Analytics & Cookie Consent

Essential session cookies keep your bag, saved wishlist, and authenticated account functioning safely. Optional analytics cookies load only if you explicitly accept them through our privacy banner.

## Your Data Rights & Anonymisation

You may review saved profile details and order records at any time from your account dashboard, or request permanent account deletion and data anonymisation. Financial order records required under statutory tax rules retain order totals while stripping personal identifiers upon deletion.
