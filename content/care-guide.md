## Daily Wear Rituals

Diamonds attract natural skin oils, lotions, and soap film that soften fire and brilliance over time. Keeping heirloom pieces luminous requires simple consistency rather than harsh abrasives:

- Put jewellery on last after applying fragrance, haircare, sunscreen, or hand creams.
- Remove rings before lifting weights, gardening, swimming in chlorinated pools, or cleaning with household bleach.
- Store each piece separately in a soft fabric-lined compartment so diamonds never scratch neighbouring 18K gold surfaces.

## Gentle Home Cleaning

1. Fill a small bowl with lukewarm water and two drops of mild, fragrance-free dish soap.
2. Soak the piece for fifteen minutes to loosen residue behind the pavilion and under pavé settings.
3. Brush gently around galleries and prongs using a soft-bristled baby toothbrush.
4. Rinse under lukewarm running water with the sink drain plugged, then pat dry with a lint-free microfibre cloth.
