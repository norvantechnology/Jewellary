## Insured Courier Delivery

All fine jewellery dispatches from our vault via insured, climate-stabilised express courier in discreet, unmarked outer packaging.

- **Complimentary Domestic Express:** Orders exceeding {{freeShippingThreshold}} qualify automatically for complimentary insured domestic delivery.
- **Signature Verification:** Orders totalling {{signatureThreshold}} or more require a direct adult signature on delivery. Couriers cannot leave parcels unattended at doorsteps or parcel lockers.
- **Dispatch Windows:** In-stock pieces leave our studio within 1 to 2 business days; made-to-order designs display their atelier crafting timeline on each piece's dossier page.

## International Duties & Jurisdictions

We currently deliver insured parcels to supported regions including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and India. Applicable shipping zones and transit estimates calculate transparently during checkout before payment.

## {{returnWindowDays}}-Day Complimentary Returns

If a creation is not quite right, unworn items in original condition—accompanied by the presentation box, security tag intact, and original GIA or IGI laboratory report—may be returned within **{{returnWindowDays}} days** of delivery.

To initiate a return authorization, contact [{{contactEmail}}](mailto:{{contactEmail}}) with your order number. An insured return label and tamper-evident shipping instructions will be sent to your inbox. Please note that bespoke commissions, custom ring sizes beyond standard range, and engraved bands cannot be returned.
