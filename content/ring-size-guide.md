## Measuring Tips for an Accurate Fit

A well-fitted band slides gently over the knuckle without twisting loosely once seated at the base of the finger. Because finger circumference fluctuates slightly with temperature and time of day:

- Measure at the end of the day when fingers are warm and at their natural resting size.
- Avoid measuring immediately after vigorous exercise or in extreme cold.
- Wide bands (4 mm and wider) fit more snugly than narrow 1.8 mm solitaires; consider sizing up one quarter to one half size for wider court bands.

## Measuring an Existing Ring

Place a well-fitting ring intended for the exact same finger onto a millimeter ruler and read the **internal diameter** directly from inside edge to inside edge (excluding the gold wall thickness).
