## Diamond Certification & Quality

### Do your diamonds include independent laboratory reports?

Yes. All centre-stone solitaires and qualified creations arrive with their physical and digital GIA or IGI grading dossier matching the laser inscription on the diamond's girdle.

### Do you offer both natural and lab-grown diamonds?

Yes. Both natural and lab-grown stones undergo identical selection criteria—prioritising Ideal/Excellent cut proportions, eye-clean clarity, and zero hazy fluorescence. Each product page states stone origin clearly.

## Sizing, Customisation & Orders

### Can engagement rings be resized after delivery?

Most cathedral and solitaire bands may be resized within one to two US sizes depending on pavé coverage. For full eternity bands, sizing cannot be altered once set, so please verify measurements with our Ring Size Guide or contact Concierge before ordering.

### How long does bespoke or made-to-order crafting take?

Ready-to-ship pieces dispatch within 1–2 business days. Creations crafted to order require the lead time stated on the product detail page, typically 14–21 calendar days.
